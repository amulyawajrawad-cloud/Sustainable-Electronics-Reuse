Reuse is a simple process

Extending component life starts with one small step.

STEP 01

📦 Collect & list

Students, colleges, repair shops, and makers submit unused electronic parts with condition and quantity details.

STEP 02

🧪 Test & verify

Components are inspected and their condition is recorded before being marked as verified for reuse.

STEP 03

♻ Reuse or recycle

Working parts find a new owner. Defective parts are directed to responsible material-recovery partners.

*Illustrative demo figures for the frontend prototype, not live project data. Orders, verification, and recycling partner integrations require a backend.
